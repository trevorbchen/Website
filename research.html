research.dir■■■

research

I like problems where a model has to find something — a molecule, an enzyme, a feature, a failure mode — with as few expensive questions as possible.

AI for science

Closed-loop discovery

A frozen LLM proposes mechanistic hypotheses; each is distilled into a domain-specific flow model that generates testable designs, and wet-lab results certify or refute it — in campaigns of ~10² measurements. ETH Zurich (Krause) + the Arnold Lab.

drug discovery

Sample-efficient generative design

Online RL fine-tuning, active learning, and reward shaping for diffusion molecule generators: the best search baseline's score with 58× fewer oracle calls (16× on proteins). NeurIPS 2026, with Genesis Molecular AI.

theory

Why diffusion models memorize

Extra latent dimensions act as a spectral buffer that delays when a diffusion model starts copying its training data (3.6× later on CelebA). ICML HiLD 2026; ICLR 2027 under review.

interpretability

Steerable sparse autoencoders

A family of SAE training objectives that steer which features a model learns — targeted feature discovery instead of seed luck. Plus SAEs on every layer of a diffusion transformer. CVPR 2027 under review.
