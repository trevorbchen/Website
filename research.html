research

I like problems where a model has to find something (a molecule, an enzyme, a feature, a failure mode) with as few expensive questions as possible.

Underneath most of it are a few questions I keep returning to. How do you explore and exploit most efficiently when each experiment costs something? How do you make reinforcement learning work in AI for science? How do you interpret a model's failure modes instead of only measuring them? Right now that mostly means diffusion models and biology.
