Trading binary prediction markets with RL

2025-08-30 · RLmarkets

Over summer 2025 I built a medium-frequency trading system for binary prediction markets — the kind where every contract settles at 0 or 1.

What I built

  • Inventory management with reinforcement learning. Holding too much of one side is the main risk, and naive execution leaks money to slippage. An RL policy that managed inventory and hedging cut execution slippage by about 30%.
  • Signals. Sentiment analysis combined with arbitrage and hedging strategies across related markets; over a 3-month window this returned a consistent ~8% per week.
  • Execution. A secure execution API with local cryptographic signing that made the trading loop about 6× faster.

Short-window returns like that say more about a market's inefficiencies than about any lasting edge, and they shrink as markets mature. The most durable thing I took away was how much of trading is engineering: latency, inventory, and not losing money on the way in and out.
